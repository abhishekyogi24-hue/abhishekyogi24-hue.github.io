2021 — present

CheapOair AI Chatbot

Post-booking flight support chatbot at cheapoair.com. Rebuilt from a rule-based Dialogflow bot into a Vertex AI multi-agent system — CSAT 62%→80%, containment 48%→53%.

Try it out ↗
Case study

Why a chatbot. Call and email were the only support channels. ~60% of bookings are international, making calls expensive and email too slow.

Phase 1. Launched on Dialogflow ES (keyword-matching NLP) at 20% automation, scaled to 50% automation, 62% CSAT, 60% engagement across self-serve flows.

Phase 2. As users expected free-form, ChatGPT-style chat, rebuilt on Vertex AI as a multi-agent system — an orchestrator plus Intent Classifier, Booking Details, Small Talk, FAQ, and Summarization agents — with digression, flow-switching, and clarification handling.

Evaluation. Offline golden/adversarial/regression suites, online LLM-as-judge scoring (groundedness, faithfulness, safety, jailbreak resistance), cleared with Legal, InfoSec, and an internal AI committee before a 5% traffic rollout.

Result. Engagement 58%→70%, containment 48%→53%, CSAT 62%→80%. Biggest challenge: prompt engineering mastery.

2023 — present

Oli — AI Trip Planner

Conversational trip-planning assistant in the CheapOair app. Owned from problem framing and PRD through a phased roadmap — +15% engagement, 65+ daily flight conversions.

Case study

Problem. Travelers discover destinations in one place, search flights in another, and lose all planning context between sessions — the app and Oli ran as two disconnected journeys.

Personas. Explorer ("I don't know where to go"), Planner ("I know where I want to go"), Shopper ("I want the best flight").

Capabilities. Destination discovery, trip planning, flight discovery, context persistence, journey intelligence, proactive engagement, all personalized via geolocation, behavioral signals, and conversation memory.

Research & execution. The mandate came from leadership: solve trip-planning discovery with AI. Started with competitive analysis of Trip.com and Mindtrip, then a month of UX iteration reviewed with leadership before locking the interaction model. Execution ran across three engineering pods — Chatbot (UI/logic), RPA (Gen AI content backend), and App (where Oli surfaces) — via daily grooming syncs.

Roadmap. Phase 1 — discovery, itineraries, flights, sharing. Phase 2 — behavior syncing, journey intelligence, personalized recs. Phase 3 — proactive notifications, recommendation optimization, deep booking-funnel integration.

Staged rollout. Internal UAT → org-wide test pass → 5% of app traffic, auditing transcripts and A/B metrics to confirm Oli wasn't cannibalizing existing app conversion — then 20% → 50% → 100% as metrics trended positive. Tracked via A/B testing, Excel/Power BI, and Google Analytics funnel data.

Result. +15% engagement, 65+ daily flight conversions.

2026

Arthritis Companion

A login-free daily PWA for tracking arthritis symptoms and gentle exercises — designed around one real person's arthritic hands. Vibe-coded solo as the PM.

Try it out ↗ View source ↗
Case study

Problem. Existing symptom trackers assume fine motor control — sliders, drag gestures, small tap targets. For someone managing arthritis, the tool meant to help was itself hard to use.

Design constraints. Large tap targets, no sliders or drag gestures, voice-friendly notes, a fixed bottom "Done" button reachable one-handed, no login or account.

Approach. Local-first: all data on-device via IndexedDB, no backend, exportable backups. Four focused screens — Today, History, Exercises, Guides.

How it was built. No engineering team — Abhishek vibe-coded it solo. His job was the product: framing the problem, setting constraints, making scope/UX calls, and reviewing every screen against a real user, with AI coding tools turning those decisions into shipped code.

Outcome. An installable, offline-capable PWA a real user opens daily to log symptoms and exercises in seconds, one-handed, with zero account friction.
